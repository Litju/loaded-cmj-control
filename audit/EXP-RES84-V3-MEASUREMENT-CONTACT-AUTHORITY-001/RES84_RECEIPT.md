### Defect closed

The RES-84A comparator confirmed one sample early: it gated on an inclusive
**sample count** (`end - start >= ceil(D/dt)`, i.e. `K_D` samples = `K_D - 1`
intervals) and reported the onset sample as the comparator time. The takeoff
confirmation admitted a stream ending as early as `t_end - dt` and closed its
window on the last sample at or before `t_end` instead of requiring the first
sample at or after the required end.

At 500 Hz (`dt = 0.002 s`, `D = 0.010 s`) the retired comparator accepted 5
samples (first-to-last 0.008 s); the corrected comparator requires
`K_D = 5` intervals and `K_D + 1 = 6` true samples (first-to-last 0.010 s).
For flight (`D = 0.050 s`) the corrected rule requires the first native sample
at or after `occurrence + 0.078 s` (sample 39 for the real hop), never sample
38 (0.076 s).

### Correction

* One shared deterministic measurement-layer primitive
  `physical_time_dwell` (`src/loaded_cmj/v3/measurement.py`) is used by both
  `force_takeoff_comparator` and `confirm_takeoff`.
* `K_D = ceil(D / dt)` and all dwell comparisons are computed in **integer
  nanoseconds** reconstructed exactly from the declared values
  (`Fraction.limit_denominator(1e9)`, `NANOSECONDS_PER_SECOND = 1e9`); runtime
  float `ceil`, floating epsilon allowances and float accumulated dwell are not
  authority. A stream that is not an exact native grid fails closed with
  `V3DwellAuthorityError`.
* The primitive reports `onset_index`, `onset_time_s`, `confirmation_index`,
  `confirmation_time_s`, `required_duration_s`, `elapsed_duration_s`, `K_D`
  (`k_d`) and `true_sample_count`, and requires
  `elapsed_duration_s >= required_duration_s` (integer nanoseconds).
* Comparator reporting now separates `COMPARATOR_ONSET_TIME`,
  `COMPARATOR_CONFIRMATION_TIME`,
  `COMPARATOR_ONSET_OFFSET_FROM_TAKEOFF` and
  `COMPARATOR_CONFIRMATION_OFFSET_FROM_TAKEOFF`; the frozen per-foot 10 N
  predicate, force source, invalidation policy and diagnostic-only status are
  unchanged.
* Confirmation sustain checks include every native sample from the first
  zero-support sample through the first sample at or after the required end
  inclusive; a missing sample is `REJECT_DWELL_COVERAGE`.
